Poppy the Kangaroo Mouse's Fish Pond is an incremental activity located in Yum-Yum Desert in which players passively catch fish. Fish can be spent on various upgrades which enhance the rate that fish are caught, or on global bonuses that impact the rest of the game.

Getting Started

To get started, players must buy the Secret Map from the Yum-Yum Grotto vendor. Using the map will give instructions on how to reach The Oasis. After locating the entrance, using the map will teleport players inside and a waypoint will be added to the map.

Mechanics

The fish pond passively generates fish every 30 seconds. Both the quantity of fish caught and the rate at which they're caught can be improved by purchasing upgrades.

Clicking on the pond awards one fish per click. Clicking too often will temporarily throttle collection in this manner to 1 fish every second.

Bonuses from Poppy

The Bonuses from Poppy upgrade grants numerous bonuses to several stats outside of the fish pond. Each upgrade level boosts a single stat in order before cycling back to the first stat.

Image Bonus per Level
+3% Fishing Efficiency
+3 Defence
+5% Fishing XP
+2% Accuracy
+2% Total DMG
+0.5% AFK Gains
+3% Cash

Megafish

Megafish give unique bonuses within the pond and increase the effect of Poppy's bonuses.

Megafish Description
Squid Megafish Unlocks the first 3 upgrades in the Tar Pit, visit through top left corner
Sequin Megafish Boosts all of Poppy's Bonuses by 1.5x their base amount
Nautilus Megafish Adds two more Reset Spirals to upgrade, and gives +5 Pts when Fisheroo Resetting
Koi Megafish Boosts all of Poppy's Bonuses by 2x their base amount.
Angler Megafish Unlocks another 3 upgrades in the Tar Pit, and boosts Tartar Fish gain by 3x
Leech Megafish The Fishing Buddy upgrade now gives +50% Bluefin caught and +50% Shiny Speed per Lv past Lv.5!
Aquaray Megafish Boosts all of Poppy's Bonuses by 2.5x their base amount
Jettison Megafish Unlocks the final 2 upgrades in the Tar Pit, and boosts Tartar Fish gain by another 3x
Shoal Megafish Boosts all of Poppy's Bonuses by 3x their base amount
Eel Megafish Shiny fishing is 1% faster per upgrade LV of the Tasty Fishbait upgrade
Marlin Megafish All upgrades, including Tar Pit upgrades, are 5% cheaper per LV of King Worm upgrade
Eclectic Megafish Poppy's bonuses are now 3.5x higher than they were at first[1]
